Legend of Zelda — P1 Gold

By Tongfei Zou and Leo Liu

A recreation of the classic Zelda dungeon flow with a four-room custom challenge, built with Unity, C#, the Unity Input System, and 2D Physics. I implemented the custom level, charged sword mechanic, projectile barrage encounters, room progression, charge HUD, and in-game teaching cues.
